## Untagged topics

This book is a formal-languages/automata-theory dissertation rather than a
type-theory, proof-theory, or constraint-solving text, so most of its Topic
List sits outside the four Focus Areas. The connection that does exist is
structural: antichain algorithms and P-automata reachability are symbolic,
lattice/fixed-point-based state-space exploration techniques of the same
flavor as CEGAR loops and abstract-interpretation reachability analysis
(`static-analysis`, `sat-smt-csp`), and the model-checking-as-inclusion
framing (Chapter 1) is the same automata-theoretic verification paradigm
that motivates Hoare-logic/CHC-based verification-condition discharge. The
remaining topics — pushdown automata foundations, superdeterministic PDAs,
visibly pushdown automata proper, and the timed-automata material — are
mostly self-contained automata-theory content with no direct payoff for the
Rust compiler / dependent-type elaborator / CSP kernel project, so they are
left untagged rather than forcing a strained connection:
- Pushdown Automata Foundations
- Superdeterministic Pushdown Automata
- Visibly Pushdown Automata
- Timed Automata and Their Pushdown Extensions
